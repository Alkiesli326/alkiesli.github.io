About Cupcakes by Amie

Cupcakes by Amie started from my love of baking and creating special treats for family and friends. What began as something I enjoyed doing for fun grew into a small business where I can share my creations with others.

One of my favorite parts of baking is coming up with different flavors and decorating cupcakes and cakes for birthdays, holidays, weddings and other special occasions. I enjoy making each creation a little different and bringing someone's idea to life.

Cupcakes by Amie is all about homemade treats, creative designs and making something special for every celebration.
