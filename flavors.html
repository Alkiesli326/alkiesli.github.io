Cupcake Flavors

Cupcakes by Amie offers a variety of cupcake flavors for celebrations, holidays or just because. Here are some of my most popular flavors.

Mississippi Mud

A rich chocolate cupcake, chocolate mousse center, whipped cream and chocolate cookie crumble.

Banana Pudding

A banana pudding inspired cupcake with pudding in the center and topped with fresh whipped cream, banana slices and vanilla wafers.

Strawberry Cheesecake

A cheesecake flavored cupcake with a creamy cheesecake filling and topped with sweet strawberry frosting and fresh strawberry slices.

Peach Cobbler

A vanilla cupcake filled with peaches cooked in rum and topped with brown sugar rum frosting and a crispy pie crumble.

Toasted Coconut

A vanilla cupcake filled with coconut cream mousse and topped with delicious toasted coconut.

Tiramisu

A vanilla cupcake filled with coffee-flavored mascarpone cream and topped with cocoa powder.

Red Velvet

A classic red velvet cupcake topped with cream cheese frosting.

Key Lime

A citrus lime cupcake with a tangy key lime pie filling and topped with whipped cream.
